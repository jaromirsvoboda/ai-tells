# Credits for the examples

Every example shown in the catalogue is listed here with its source, author and licence. Images were cropped, marked with a thin white-and-black outline where the tell is, downscaled to at most 900 px wide (video frames 600 px) and re-encoded as WebP, which strips metadata; nothing else was changed. Real examples come from Wikimedia Commons (licences as stated on each file page) and from the examples quoted in Wikipedia's guide *Signs of AI writing* (CC BY-SA 4.0; the quoted edits are by Wikipedia contributors, and the diff link identifies each one). Mock-ups, generated examples and code snippets marked “made for this catalogue” are released under CC0. AI output is generally not protected by copyright; the licence stated by the uploader is given where one exists.
